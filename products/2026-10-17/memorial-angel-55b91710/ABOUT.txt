In Loving Memory 3D Shadow-Box Memorial - 7 Layers

A keepsake shadow box for someone you will never stop missing.

PERSONALISE THE NAME PLATE (the front layer):
  1. Open memorial-angel-55b91710-design-1-layer-1.svg in Cricut Design Space or
     Silhouette Studio.
  2. Double-click the placeholder text and type your beloved's name
     and dates. Everything else cuts as-is.

ASSEMBLE: cut each layer from cardstock, stack largest to smallest
with foam dots. Voids in the upper layers reveal the layers beneath
- that is the depth of the keepsake.
