Memorial Shadow Box for Cat

Losing a cat is losing a family member. Here's how to make a memorial shadow box for your cat:

1. Choose the paw print motif

The paw print works for cats too - tabby, siamese, maine coon, and every other breed. It's instantly recognizable.

2. Edit your cat's name

The front layer has an editable name plate. Type your cat's name and their birth and death years.

3. Cut and stack

Cut all 7 layers from cardstock. Stack largest to smallest with foam tape. The paw print sits at the heart of the shadow box.

4. Where to hang it

Near their favorite window, above their feeding spot, or on a bedroom shelf. Wherever they used to be.
