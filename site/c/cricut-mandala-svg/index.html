Buying guide

Importing into Cricut Design Space

Upload the SVG directly rather than converting it to an image. Design Space accepts SVG upload, and keeping it as vector means the cut paths stay exact. After uploading, confirm the size reads correctly at the top of the canvas, then ungroup only if you need to move individual layers independently.

Why one file per layer matters

Each layer ships as its own SVG so you can cut it on its own sheet. If everything arrived as one flattened file you would have to manually separate the tones by colour, which is slow and error-prone. Cut in ascending layer order and keep the pieces labelled as you go.

Adhesive and alignment

A thin dab of craft glue or a strip of foam tape on the back of each layer is enough. Too much adhesive causes the layers to sink into each other and the depth disappears. Align from the centre outwards rather than corner to corner, so small errors average out instead of accumulating across the sheet.
