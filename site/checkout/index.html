How it works

  1. Find your bundle below and note the exact amount shown - each one is unique so your payment can be matched to your order.
  2. Send exactly that amount of USDT (BEP20) to the address shown.
  3. Wait a few minutes. The payment is detected automatically on-chain, then your download link appears on your order page. Nothing to sign up for, no account, no login.

Send only USDT (BEP20) on the BEP20 network (BSC). Other assets sent here are unrecoverable.

No crypto wallet? Here is how to pay in 3 minutes
  1. Open any crypto exchange account you already have (Binance, Coinbase, Crypto.com, Kraken, or a wallet app like Trust Wallet or MetaMask).
  2. Buy USDT and send it on the BEP20 / BSC network. Some apps label it "BNB Smart Chain" or "BSC" - that is the same one. Do NOT send on Ethereum (ERC20) or Tron (TRC20).
  3. Send exactly the amount in the table for your bundle. Those last digits identify your order; a different amount cannot be matched automatically.

Once the transfer confirms on-chain (usually under 5 minutes) your order page flips to paid and the download unlocks by itself. There is nothing to claim and no account to create.
